# Scope

## Supported in MVP

- one fully configured jurisdiction (Morocco/MA);
- a second jurisdiction only if time permits;
- two contract types: NDA and Service Agreement;
- one primary language (French) and one optional secondary language (English);
- AI natural-language input;
- guided structured input;
- legal-source administration;
- clause library;
- hybrid RAG;
- contract planning;
- structured generation;
- deterministic validation;
- AI risk review;
- interactive editing;
- controlled negotiation;
- versioning;
- approval;
- DOCX/PDF/HTML export;
- evaluation console.

## Excluded initially

- many contract types with shallow configuration;
- general legal advice;
- autonomous negotiation;
- full CLM platform;
- compliance claims for all MENA countries.
